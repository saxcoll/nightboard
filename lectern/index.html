Curatorial Thesis & Selection Standards

Lectern Canon · High-Density Standard

The Standard of Density

Lectern is built for students and researchers who learn best when material is rigorous, mathematically transparent, and unapologetically technical. Grant Sanderson's (3Blue1Brown) and Welch Labs' work defines our standard of density: content that does not shy away from proofs, matrices, and formal mechanics.

What is Included (IN)

University lecture series (MIT OpenCourseWare, Stanford, Oxford, Yale, Gresham College, IAS), deep algorithmic architectures (Karpathy, Ben Eater, Reducible, Polylog, Jon Gjengset), pure mathematical foundations (Mathologer, Aleph 0, Morphocular, Richard Behiel), and foundational humanities (Michael Sugrue, Jeffrey Kaplan, Historia Civilis, Fall of Civilizations).

What is Excluded (OUT)

Short-form sensationalism, superficial pop-science (Kurzgesagt, Veritasium, Crash Course, Two Minute Papers), tech hype mills, TED talks, and all music theory channels (Adam Neely, Rick Beato, 12tone). Videos under 12 minutes and Shorts are discarded by default.
